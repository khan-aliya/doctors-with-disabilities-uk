Get in Touch

Whether you want to share your story, suggest a resource, flag something we've got wrong, or explore collaborating, please reach out. All messages are read personally. This page provides general peer‑support information and does not reproduce BMA guidance.

Ways to get involved

Share your story

First-person accounts from doctors and medical students with any condition or disability. Published with written consent. Anonymous options available. You review everything before it's published.

Suggest a resource

Know of a useful organisation, scheme, or piece of guidance we haven't listed? Tell us and we'll review it for inclusion.

Collaborate

LMCs, deaneries, RCGP, BMA, NHS organisations, or individuals, we're open to partnerships and joint projects that help disabled doctors.

Feedback or correction

If something on this site is wrong, outdated, or unclear - please tell us. We'd rather know and fix it.

Send us a message

All messages are read personally by the team. We aim to respond within one week. For urgent employment or legal situations, please contact the BMA directly.

If you're sharing a story, a brief description here is fine - we'll follow up by email to discuss the full process.

Your data is safe. We collect only what you provide here. Your information is used solely to respond to your message. We do not share it with third parties, sell it, or use it for marketing. You have the right to request deletion at any time, email us or see our Privacy Policy for details. This form is processed by Netlify Forms in accordance with their privacy policy.
