Regional - Shropshire & West Midlands

National guidance only gets you so far. Knowing who to actually call in your region - the right person at the LMC, the right contact at the deanery - makes a bigger practical difference than any policy document. This section covers Shropshire and the West Midlands and will grow as we hear from more local doctors.

Shropshire, Telford & Wrekin LMC

The STW LMC represents GPs and primary care doctors in Shropshire, Telford and Wrekin. If you're a GP or GP trainee in the area dealing with an employment dispute, an adjustment that won't move, or a contractual issue you can't resolve through normal channels - your LMC is the first call to make.

Shropshire, Telford & Wrekin LMC

Your local LMC - representing GPs and primary care doctors in the STW area. Contact them for local employment support and advice.

NHS England West Midlands - Training Support

NHS England's West Midlands office oversees postgraduate medical training across the region. If your TPD isn't responding adequately and you've already tried raising it directly, escalating to the NHS England West Midlands postgraduate team is the next step. Put everything in writing before you do.

NHS England - West Midlands

Postgraduate medical training in the West Midlands - contacts for trainee support, LTFT applications, and training adjustments.
